All your game and app accounts, locked on your own PC.

NEXORA is a Windows app that keeps the logins for your games, launchers and apps in an encrypted vault protected by your master password. It fills in sign-ins for you and, for teams, shares accounts without the server ever seeing a password.

Ask for early access

What it does

Encrypted vault

Accounts, passwords and notes are encrypted on your PC with a key made from your master password. Nobody else holds that key.

Quick switcher

A shortcut opens a small search box over whatever you're doing, so you can copy a login in a second.

Sign-in for you

Opens the Riot Client and fills in the account you picked. Steam and Epic get a ready-to-paste sequence.

Backups

Encrypted backups to a folder or to your Google Drive, with versions you can restore.

Work vaults (Pro)

A business shares accounts with its staff. Each account can be limited to chosen people, and the owner approves every change.

Your look

Light and dark themes, accent colours, compact mode, and your own icons for games and apps.

How NEXORA uses your Google account

Sign in with Google

Optional. NEXORA receives your name, email address and profile picture only to create and sign in to your NEXORA account.

Google Drive backups

Optional. NEXORA can only see files it created itself. It uploads your backups there, already encrypted, and nothing else.

Details are in the privacy policy. NEXORA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
